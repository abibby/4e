Rogue Attack 1Opening Move

While your enemies fumble for weapons or stand and gape, you leap into the fray to score a deadly hit.

Encounter        Martial, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Requirement: You must be wielding a light blade.

Target: One creature

Attack: Dexterity vs. AC

Hit: 2[W] + Dexterity modifier damage.

Effect: You gain a power bonus to AC and Reflex equal to your Charisma modifier until the end of your next turn.

  Artful Dodger: The defense bonus equals 2 + your Charisma modifier.


Published in Dragon Magazine 381, page(s) 68.
