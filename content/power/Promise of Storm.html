Genasi Racial PowerPromise of Storm

The lightning living within you calls out to its companion, the thunder. Sparks dance across your skin, and the air around you seems to darken and rumble.

Encounter        Lightning, Thunder
Minor Action      Personal

Effect: Until the end of your next turn, you deal 1d8 extra damage when you hit with a thunder attack or a lightning attack.
At 11th level, increase your extra damage to 2d8. At 21st level, increase your extra damage to 3d8.


Revision (2/10/2009)
In the Effect line, replace the first sentence with “Until the end of your next turn, you deal 1d8 extra damage when you hit with a thunder attack or a lightning attack.”
