Ardent Feature Ardent Eruption

Your pain bleeds from you in a wave, touching your allies and lending new ferocity to their attacks.

Encounter        Psionic
No Action      Close burst 5

Trigger: You are bloodied by an attack

Target: Each ally in the burst

Effect: The target gains a +2 bonus to his or her damage rolls until the start of your next turn.
Level 11: +4 bonus.
Level 21: +6 bonus.
