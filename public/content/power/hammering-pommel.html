Fighter Attack 25Hammering Pommel

You strike with your weapon and then use the weapon’s pommel to knock your opponent backward, rattling its senses.

Daily        Martial, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Requirement: You must have a hand free.

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. AC

Hit: 4[W] + Strength modifier damage, and you push the target 3 squares. The target is stunned (save ends).

Miss: Half damage, and you push the target 1 square. The target is stunned until the end of your next turn.


Published in Martial Power 2, page(s) 20.
