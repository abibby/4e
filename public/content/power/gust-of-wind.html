Druid Attack 1Gust of Wind

A blast of air buffets nearby creatures, hurling them backward as it drives them to the ground.

Encounter        Implement, Primal
Standard Action      Close blast 3

Target: Each creature in the blast

Attack: Wisdom vs. Fortitude

Hit: 1d8 + Wisdom modifier damage, and you knock the target prone.

Effect: You push the target up to 2 squares.


Published in Heroes of the Feywild, page(s) 78.
