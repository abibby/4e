Wizard Utility 2Charm of Protection

The instant before a blow connects, your spell interposes a spectral object in its path.

Encounter        Arcane
Immediate Interrupt      Personal

Trigger: You are hit by an attack.

Effect: You gain a +2 power bonus to all defenses against the attack, and you gain temporary hit points equal to 2 + your Wisdom modifier.


Published in Heroes of the Feywild, page(s) 93.
