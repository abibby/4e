Great Fortitude

Benefit: You gain a +2 feat bonus to Fortitude. This bonus increases to +3 at 11th level and +4 at 21st level.


Update (8/5/2010)
Feat updated to match Essentials data.

Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 203, Heroes of the Fallen Lands, page(s) 314, Heroes of the Forgotten Kingdoms, page(s) 314.
