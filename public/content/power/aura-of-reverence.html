Bard Utility 22Aura of Reverence

Enemies cringe before you, skulking away lest you turn your attention to them.

Daily        Arcane, Fear
Minor Action      Personal

Effect: Until the end of the encounter, your skald’s aura gains the following effect: Any bloodied enemy that starts its turn in the aura must use the first action of its turn to move out of the aura, if possible.


Published in Heroes of the Feywild, page(s) 66.
