Fighter Utility 6Makeshift Shield

You snatch a piece of furniture, a body, or a discarded item to block an enemy’s attack.

Encounter        Martial
Immediate Interrupt      Personal

Requirement: You must have a hand free.

Trigger: An enemy hits you with a close or a melee attack

Effect: You gain a a bonus to defenses against the attack equal to your Dexterity modifer.


Published in Martial Power 2, page(s) 11.
