Fighter Attack 9Hobbling Cut

You cripple your foe with painful wounds to its leg and its arm.

Daily        Martial, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. AC

Hit: 2[W] + Strength modifier damage, and the target is slowed and weakened (save ends both).

Miss: Half damage, and the target is slowed and weakened until the end of your next turn.


Published in Martial Power 2, page(s) 13.
