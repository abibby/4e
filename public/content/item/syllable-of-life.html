Syllable of LifeLevel 13 Uncommon

When Hoon shines, you feel vigorous and youthful, as if no hurt could ever stop you.

Consumable: Consumable        650 gp

Power Consumable (Standard Action)

Requirement: You must be able to speak Abyssal or Supernal.

Effect: You speak the syllable and it orbits your head. Until the end of the encounter you gain regeneration 10 while you are bloodied.



Published in Dragon Magazine 392.
