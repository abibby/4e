Barbarian Attack 15Scytheclaw Rage

You batter a foe to the ground, and you channel the spirit of the scytheclaw drake. Even as you swing your weapon, you kick and punch to keep your foes back.

Daily        Primal, Rage, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. AC

Hit: 3[W] + Strength modifier damage.

Miss: Half damage.

Effect: You knock the target prone. You enter the rage of the scytheclaw drake. Until the rage ends, you can push an enemy adjacent to you 2 squares as a minor action once per round.


Published in Primal Power, page(s) 18.
