Cleric Attack 13Plague of Doom

You whisper an ancient battle prayer and send jolts of wracking pain through your enemy to weaken its defenses.

Encounter        Divine, Implement
Standard Action      Ranged 10

Target: One creature

Attack: Wisdom vs. Fortitude

Hit: 3d8 + Wisdom modifier damage, and the target takes a penalty to all defenses until the end of your next turn. The penalty equals your Charisma modifier.
