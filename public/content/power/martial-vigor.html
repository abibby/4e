Warlord Utility 6Martial Vigor

When your attack connects, you experience a burst of confidence your allies can share if they follow your lead.

Encounter        Healing, Martial
Free Action      Personal

Trigger: You hit an enemy with a martial melee or ranged attack

Effect: You can spend a healing surge. Until the start of your next turn, any ally that hits the same target with an attack gained from an action point can also spend a healing surge.


Published in Dragon Magazine 384, page(s) 51.
