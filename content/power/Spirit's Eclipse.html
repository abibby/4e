Sorcerer Utility 10Spirit's Eclipse

A mental bulwark helps to protect you against your foe’s attack and renders the creature incapable of seeing you for a time.

Encounter        Arcane
Immediate Interrupt      Personal

Trigger: An enemy hits you with an attack that targets Will

Effect: Until the end of your next turn, you gain a +5 bonus to Will and are invisible to the triggering enemy.


Published in Arcane Power, page(s) 34.
