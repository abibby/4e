Dread of Sakkors

Heroic Tier
Prerequisite: Shadar-kai, shadow jaunt racial power
Benefit: You gain a +2 feat bonus to Intimidate checks.
    When you hit an enemy with an attack while you are insubstantial, you can push that enemy up to 2 squares. The enemy then becomes slowed until the end of your next turn.

Published in Dragon Magazine 391.
