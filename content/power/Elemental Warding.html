Sorcerer Utility 10Elemental Warding

You ward an area with seething elemental power.

Encounter        Arcane, Elemental, Zone
Minor Action      Close burst 10

Target: One, two, or three squares in the burst

Effect: Choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder. Each target square becomes a zone that lasts until the end of the encounter. You or an ally gains resist 10 to damage of the chosen type while in the zone.
