Potion of Cure Moderate WoundsLevel 10 Uncommon

This potion covers your wounds in silver light, helping them heal.

Consumable: Potion        200 gp

Utility Power (Healing) Consumable (Minor Action)

Effect: You drink the potion. If you have a healing surge, you must spend one. Instead of the hit points you would normally regain, you regain 2d8 + 10 hit points. If you are bloodied and don’t have any healing surges, you still regain the hit points. If neither of these things is true, there is no effect.



Published in Mordenkainen's Magnificent Emporium, page(s) 96.
