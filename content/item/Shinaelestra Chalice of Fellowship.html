Shinaelestra Chalice of FellowshipLevel 9 Common

You make a toast with this chalice to honor heroism and friendship, bolstering those who share the toast.

Wondrous Item        4,200 gp

Utility Power Daily (Standard Action)

Effect: You raise the chalice in a toast, and each ally within 10 squares of you can speak fair words of his or her companions’ deeds. Those who do so and then take an extended rest (without having an intervening encounter) gain 5 temporary hit points at the end of the extended rest. For each additional chalice present during the toast, the temporary hit points granted increase by 2, to a maximum of 15.



Published in Heroes of the Feywild, page(s) 137.
