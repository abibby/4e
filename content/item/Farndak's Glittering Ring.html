Farndak's Glittering RingLevel 22 Uncommon

This gem-studded ring lets you draw vigor from your allies even as it pulls a foe into your grasp.

Ring Slot        325,000 gp

Property

When you spend a healing surge, you regain additional hit points equal to the number of allies who wear one or more rings from this set.
If you’ve reached at least one milestone today, double the number of additional hit points you regain from this property.

Power (Charm) Daily (Minor Action)

Choose one enemy within 5 squares of you. You pull the chosen enemy 5 squares and mark it (save ends).
