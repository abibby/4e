Fighter Attack 1Goading Maneuver

After a swift strike, you step to the side and draw another one or two foes into your trap.

Encounter        Martial, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. AC

Hit: 2[W] + Strength modifier damage, and you shift 2 squares. In addition, you mark one or two enemies adjacent to you until the end of your next turn.


Published in Dark Sun Campaign Setting, page(s) 87.
