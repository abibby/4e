Sorcerer Utility 2Spirit Guidance

You allow your magic to flow toward your familiar so that your next attack is more accurate.

Encounter        Arcane
Minor Action      Personal

Requirement: Your familiar must be in its active mode.

Effect: Until the end of your next turn, you gain a +2 power bonus to attack rolls against any creature adjacent to your familiar.


Published in Dragon Magazine 386, page(s) 21.
