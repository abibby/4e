Warlord Attack 9Shift the Field

Swinging your weapon calls your allies to strike. If the plan works, you all adjust your positions to respond the battlefield’s new developments.

Daily        Martial, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. AC

Hit: 3[W] + Strength modifier damage.

Miss: Half damage.

Effect: Choose two allies within 5 squares of you that can both see and hear you. Each ally makes a basic attack as a free action against different creatures that are not the targets of this attack.


Published in Dragon Magazine 384, page(s) 55.
