Spellscarred Utility 16Healing Flesh of the Infected

A hunk of flesh torn from your body infuses an ally with life.

Encounter  
Standard Action      Melee touch

Target: One creature

Effect: You spend a healing surge but regain no hit points from it. Instead, the target can regain hit points as if it had spent a healing surge and gains an equal number of temporary hit points.


Published in Forgotten Realms Player's Guide, page(s) 44.
