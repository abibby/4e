Invoker Attack 23Storm of Celestia

You invoke the storms that sometimes rumble over Celestia’s slopes, using their winds and thunder to scatter the enemy.

Encounter        Divine, Implement, Thunder
Standard Action      Area burst 2 within 10 squares

Target: Each enemy in the burst

Attack: Wisdom vs. Fortitude

Hit: 5d6 + Wisdom modifier thunder damage, and you slide the target to any unoccupied space in the burst.


Published in Player's Handbook 2, page(s) 111.
