Arcane Battlemaster Utility 12Transmuter's Boon

You adjust the nature of nearby elemental forces to spare your ally from harm.

Encounter        Arcane
Immediate Interrupt      Close burst 5

Trigger: An ally within 5 squares of you takes acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage

Target: Each ally in the burst

Effect: Each target gains resistance to the triggering damage type equal to your Intelligence modifier until the end of your next turn.


Published in Martial Power 2, page(s) 96.
