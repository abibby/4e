Ranger Utility 22Stepping Through the Veil

As you step away from your enemy, you fade from its view, shielded by your primal magic as you prepare to strike again.

Daily        Primal
Minor Action      Personal

Effect: Until the end of the encounter, whenever you end your turn not adjacent to any enemies, you are invisible until the start of your next turn.
