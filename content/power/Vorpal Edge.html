Artificer Utility 16Vorpal Edge

The deadly parts of the weapon you touch become suffused with magic, making them sharper and deadlier.

Daily        Arcane
Minor Action      Melee touch

Target: One melee weapon

Effect: Until the end of the encounter, attacks with the target score critical hits on rolls of 18 or higher.


Published in Eberron Player's Guide, page(s) 53.
