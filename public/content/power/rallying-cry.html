Purple Dragon Knight Utility 12Rallying Cry

You shout an inspiring war cry, and your voice rings above the din of battle. Your allies surge into the fight.

Encounter        Martial
Minor Action      Close burst 5

Targets: You and each ally in the burst

Effect: Until the end of your next turn, all targets gain a +2 power bonus to speed and a +1 power bonus to saving throws.


Published in Forgotten Realms Player's Guide, page(s) 60.
