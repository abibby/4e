Warlock Attack Gaze into Nothing

You pass your sword before your foe and starlike motes of darkness leap from it to enter your enemy’s eyes, leaving them black voids.

Encounter        Arcane
Immediate Reaction      Melee 1

Requirement: You must be holding your starshadow blade.

Trigger: An adjacent enemy attacks you.

Target: The triggering enemy

Effect: The target is blinded until the end of your next turn.


Published in Dragon Magazine 393.
