Reaping Infusion

Heroic Tier
Prerequisite: Revenant, dark reaping racial power, artificer, Healing Infusion class feature
Benefit: When you use your dark reaping racial power, one ally benefiting from your resistive formula or shielding elixir deals 1d6 extra necrotic damage on its next attack before the end of your next turn.

Published in Dragon Magazine 387, page(s) 35.
