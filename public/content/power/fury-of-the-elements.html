Feat UtilityFury of the Elements

You channel elemental power and unleash its fury against your foes.

Encounter      Varies
Immediate Interrupt      Personal

Trigger: You are hit by an enemy attack that deals cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage.

Effect: You take half damage from the triggering attack. Until the end of your next turn, your next attack deals 1d6 extra damage of the type or types dealt by the triggering attack.


Published in Dragon Magazine 393.
