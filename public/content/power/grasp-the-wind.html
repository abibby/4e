Monk Utility 2Grasp the Wind

Before your enemy can force you back, you whirl around, using the power of its attack to propel you where you want to go.

Encounter        Psionic
Immediate Interrupt      Personal

Trigger: You are pulled, pushed, or slid

Effect: Instead of being affected by the forced movement, you shift the number of squares it would have moved you.


Published in Player's Handbook 3, page(s) 67.
