Barbarian Attack 1Bloodseeker's Rage

As you strike, flashes of spectral teeth and blood-red hide reveal the bloodseeker drake predator spirit that inspires your rage.

Daily        Primal, Rage, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. AC

Hit: 3[W] + Strength modifier damage.

Miss: Half damage.

Effect: You enter the rage of the bloodseeker. Until the rage ends, you can make an opportunity attack against any bloodied enemy adjacent to you that shifts.


Published in Dragon Magazine 380, page(s) 34.
