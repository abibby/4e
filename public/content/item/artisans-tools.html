Artisan’s Tools


Category: Gear
Price: 5 gp
Weight: 5 lb
Description: This collection of simple hand tools, nails, and a measuring stick is often seen in the home of a city-dwelling crafter, but adventurers would do well to take a set of these tools along. With them you can perform minor repairs, fashion basic wooden or metal objects, or dismantle simple locks.

Published in Into the Unknown: The Dungeon Survival Handbook, page(s) 114.
