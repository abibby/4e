Quaal's Feather SailLevel 12 Uncommon

With a wave of this feather, a sail unfurls from its tip, ready to be lashed to mast and ropes.

Consumable        500 gp

Utility Power Consumable (Minor Action)

Effect: You produce a sail that, once installed on a ship, can move it at a normal sailing speed even in the absence of wind. The sail persists for 24 hours.



Published in Mordenkainen's Magnificent Emporium, page(s) 100.
