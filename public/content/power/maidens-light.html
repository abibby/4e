Moon Hunter Utility 12Maiden’s Light

You shed a bright silver light that reveals your enemies and disturbs those not in their natural forms.

Encounter        Arcane, Aura
Minor Action      Personal

Effect: You activate an aura 3 of bright light that lasts until the end of your next turn. Enemies that have the shapechanger subtype or are subject to a polymorph effect grant combat advantage while in the aura.

Sustain Minor: The aura persists until the end of your next turn.


Published in Dragon Magazine 406.
