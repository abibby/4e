Invoker Utility 10Angelic Visage

As your foe attacks, you transform into the image of a death angel. Filled with terror, the foe backs away.

Encounter        Divine, Fear
Immediate Interrupt      Close burst 5

Trigger: An enemy within 5 squares of you makes an attack roll against you

Target: The triggering enemy

Effect: The target takes a -2 penalty to the triggering attack roll. After the attack is resolved, you push the target 3 squares.


Published in Player's Handbook 2, page(s) 108.
