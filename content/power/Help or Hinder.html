Warlord Utility 16Help or Hinder

You offer words of encouragement to an ally or shout words of discouragement to an enemy.

Encounter        Martial
Immediate Interrupt      Close burst 5

Trigger: An enemy within 5 squares of you saves, or an ally within 5 squares of you fails a saving throw

Target: The triggering enemy or ally in the burst

Effect: The target rerolls the saving throw. If the target is an enemy, it takes a penalty to the saving throw equal to your Intelligence modifier or Wisdom modifier. If the target is an ally, he or she gains a bonus to the saving throw equal to your Intelligence modifier or Wisdom modifier.


Published in Martial Power 2, page(s) 91.
