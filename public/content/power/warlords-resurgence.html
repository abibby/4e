Warlord Attack 25Warlord's Resurgence

With deific speed and precision, you call out an opening for an ally’s attack. You also spot an opportunity that you can later exploit.

Daily        Martial
Standard Action      Close burst 10

Target: One ally in the burst

Effect: As a free action, the target can make an attack using an encounter attack power that he or she has already used during this encounter, and the target gains a +2 bonus to the attack roll. Also, if you have used all your encounter attack powers, you regain the use of a warlord encounter attack power you have used during this encounter.


Published in Martial Power, page(s) 116.
