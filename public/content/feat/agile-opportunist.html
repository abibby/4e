Agile Opportunist

Paragon Tier
Prerequisite: 11th level
Benefit: When you are pulled, pushed, or slid into a square adjacent to an enemy, you can use an immediate reaction to make a melee basic attack against that enemy.


Revision (10/13/2009)
On the Benefit line, replace “opportunity action” with “immediate reaction.”

Published in Player's Handbook 2, page(s) 190.
