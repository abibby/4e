Chainmail


Description: Metal rings woven together into a shirt, leggings, and a hood make up a suit of chainmail. Chainmail grants good protection, but it’s cumbersome, so it reduces your mobility and agility.
    Magic appears at higher item levels, and grants higher armor bonuses than its mundane counterparts. Chainmail grants +8 AC at levels 11-15, +9 AC at levels 16-20, +10 AC at levels 21-25, and +12 AC at levels 26-30.
AC Bonus: +6
Check: -1
Speed: -1
Weight: 40 lb.
Cost: 40 gp.
Type: Chainmail

Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 214, Heroes of the Fallen Lands, page(s) 325.
