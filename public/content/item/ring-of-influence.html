Ring of InfluenceLevel 21 Uncommon

This ornate white gold and sapphire ring grants you sway over the minds of others.

Ring Slot        225,000 gp

Property

You gain a +2 item bonus to Charisma-based skill checks and ability checks.

Power Daily (Minor Action)

Until the end of your next turn, you gain a +2 power bonus to attack rolls against Will.

If you've reached at least one milestone today, the bonus to attack rolls increases to +4.



Published in Adventurer's Vault 2, page(s) 72.
