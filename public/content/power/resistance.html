Wizard Utility 10Resistance

You make yourself or another creature in range resistant to a particular kind of damage.

Daily        Arcane
Minor Action      Ranged 10

Target: You or one creature

Effect: Choose acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder. The target gains resistance to that damage type until the end of the encounter. The resistance equals your level + your Intelligence modifier.
