Barbarian Attack 3Shatterbone Strike

The fury of your assault knocks your foe off balance, leaving a hole in its defenses.

Encounter        Primal, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. AC

Hit: 2[W] + Strength modifier damage, and the target takes a -2 penalty to AC until the end of your next turn.

  Thaneborn Triumph: The penalty to AC equals your Charisma modifier.


Published in Player's Handbook 2, page(s) 53.
