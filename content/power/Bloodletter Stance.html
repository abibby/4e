Fighter Attack 19Bloodletter Stance

You strike at vital spots to draw your foe’s blood. With each hit, you aggravate the wounds and slowly destroy your enemy.

Daily        Martial, Stance, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. AC

Hit: 3[W] + Strength modifier damage.

Miss: Half damage.

Effect: The target takes ongoing 10 damage (save ends). In addition, you can assume the stance of the bloodletter. Until the stance ends, whenever you hit an enemy with a melee weapon attack, that enemy takes ongoing 5 damage (save ends).


Published in Dark Sun Campaign Setting, page(s) 89.
