Artificer Attack 3Force Infusion

Your attack binds energy within your foe’s form. The magic reacts with concussive force each time you or an ally strikes the creature.

Encounter        Arcane, Force, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee or Ranged weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Intelligence vs. Fortitude

Hit: 1[W] + Intelligence modifier + Constitution modifier force damage.

Effect: Until the end of your next turn, whenever any ally hits the target with a melee attack, that ally pushes the target 1 square.


Published in Eberron Player's Guide, page(s) 49.
