Tongs


Category: Gear
Price: 3 gp
Weight: 2 lb
Description: These foot-long metal pincers are similar to those used in smithies. With them, you can manipulate objects at times when using your hands is too risky, such as when you’re reaching into a scalding brazier, an unknown liquid, or a dark hole.

Published in Into the Unknown: The Dungeon Survival Handbook, page(s) 116.
