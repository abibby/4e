Syllable of ThoughtLevel 4 Uncommon

Naen's shining beacon clarifies your thoughts, helping you to anticipate danger.

Consumable: Consumable        40 gp

Power Consumable (Standard Action)

Requirement: You must be able to speak Abyssal or Supernal.

Effect: You speak the syllable and it orbits your head. Until the end of the encounter, you never grant combat advantage and you gain a +2 bonus to opportunity attacks.



Published in Dragon Magazine 392.
