Monk Attack 9Relentless Hound Technique

You make a quick attack against your foe, marking it with a psionic tag. Where it goes, you can follow.

Daily        Implement, Psionic
Standard Action      Melee touch

Target: One creature

Primary Attack: Dexterity vs. Reflex

Hit: 2d10 + Dexterity modifier damage.

Miss: Half damage.

Effect: Until the end of your next turn, when the target willingly leaves a square adjacent to you, you can shift 1 square as a free action. You can use the Relentless Hound Attack power once before the end of the encounter.
