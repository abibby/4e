Sage of the Swan Tower Utility 26Knowledge Applied

Tapping into the knowledge contained in the Swan Tower, you guide an ally’s actions toward success.

Encounter        Arcane, Divine
Free Action      Close burst 5

Trigger: An ally in the burst fails a skill check, an ability check, or a saving throw.

Effect: The triggering ally can make a new roll, with a power bonus equal to your Intelligence or Wisdom modifier. The ally can use either roll.


Published in Dragon Magazine 397.
