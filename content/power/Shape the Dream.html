Wizard of the Spiral Tower Utility 12Shape the Dream

You alter reality slightly, so that an attack your foe assumed it had made didn’t actually happen.

Daily        Arcane
Immediate Interrupt      Personal

Trigger: You are hit by an attack against your Will.

Effect: The attack has no effect on you.
