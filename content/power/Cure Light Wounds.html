Cleric Utility 2Cure Light Wounds

You utter a simple prayer, and your touch momentarily suffuses you or a wounded creature with a dim silver light that heals minor injuries.

Daily        Divine, Healing
Standard Action      Melee touch

Target: You or one creature

Effect: The target regains hit points as if it had spent a healing surge.
