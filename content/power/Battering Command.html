Warlord Attack 3Battering Command

You slam your weapon into your foe’s gut and cause the creature to double over, enabling an ally to smash it.

Encounter        Martial, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. Fortitude

Hit: You knock the target prone.

Effect: One ally adjacent to the target can make a melee basic attack against it as a free action.


Published in Martial Power 2, page(s) 86.
