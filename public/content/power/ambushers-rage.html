Barbarian Attack 5Ambusher's Rage

Blood calls. The ambush drake’s throaty roar erupts from your throat as you fling yourself around your wounded enemy in a dizzying set-up for the death blow.

Daily        Primal, Rage, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. AC

Hit: 3[W] + Strength modifier damage

Miss: Half damage.

Effect: You enter the rage of the ambusher. Until the rage ends, you can shift 2 squares as a minor action as long as you end the shift adjacent to a bloodied enemy.


Published in Dragon Magazine 380, page(s) 34.
