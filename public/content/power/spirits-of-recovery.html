Shaman Utility 22Spirits of Recovery

Luminous spirits of ancestral tribal healers appear around your allies, tending their wounds.

Encounter        Primal
Minor Action      Close burst 2

Target: Each ally in the burst

Effect: Each target can make a saving throw against an effect that a save can end. A target who saves gains 1d10 + your Wisdom modifier temporary hit points.
