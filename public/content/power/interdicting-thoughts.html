Psion Utility 22Interdicting Thoughts

The power of your mind forms a shield protecting you and your allies from attack, even as it slows your foes.

Encounter        Psionic, Zone
Minor Action      Close burst 3

Effect: The burst creates a zone that lasts until the end of your next turn. The zone moves with you, remaining centered on you. While within the zone, allies gain a +3 power bonus to all defenses. The zone is difficult terrain for your enemies.


Published in Psionic Power, page(s) 96.
