Boots of Striding and SpringingLevel 14 Uncommon

These enchanted boots increase your speed if you wear light armor or no armor and enhance your jumping capability.

Feet Slot        21,000 gp

Properties

  • You gain a +1 item bonus to speed while wearing light or no armor.
  • You gain a +5 item bonus to Athletics checks made to jump.


Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 246, Dungeon Master's Kit, page(s) 264.
