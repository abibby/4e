Cleric Attack 25Subjugating Strike

As your weapon crashes into your foe, a pulse of divine force smashes into enemies that thought they had you surrounded.

Daily        Divine, Force, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Wisdom vs. AC

Hit: 4[W] + Wisdom modifier force damage, and the target is dazed (save ends).

Miss: Half damage.

Effect: Each enemy within 2 squares of the target takes 10 force damage.
