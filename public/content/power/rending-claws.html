Druid Attack 13Rending Claws

A slash with your claws reveals your foe’s weaknesses.

Encounter        Beast Form, Implement, Primal
Standard Action      Melee touch

Target: One creature

Attack: Wisdom vs. Reflex

Hit: 2d8 + Wisdom modifier damage, and the target gains vulnerable 5 to all damage until the end of your next turn.

  Primal Predator: The vulnerability equals 3 + your Dexterity modifier.


Published in Primal Power, page(s) 45.
