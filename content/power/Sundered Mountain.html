Monk Attack 15Sundered Mountain

A cracking noise sounds when you strike your foe, and the creature’s staggering, pain-wracked steps are evidence of the extent of its injuries.

Daily        Implement, Psionic
Standard Action      Melee touch

Target: One creature

Attack: Dexterity vs. Fortitude

Hit: 4d10 + Dexterity modifier damage.

Miss: Half damage.

Effect: The target is subjected to sundering (save ends). Until this effect ends, the target takes 5 damage for each square it willingly enters. If the target moves more than half its speed willingly, it falls prone at the end of its movement. While you are adjacent to the target, it cannot make saving throws to end this effect.


Published in Heroes of the Elemental Chaos, page(s) 78.
