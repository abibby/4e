Electrified Floor
Object
Level 10 TrapXP 500

Detect Perception or Arcana DC 26

Initiative -

Immune attacks

Triggered Actions

Attack (lightning) At-Will

Trigger: A creature enters an electrified square or starts its turn there.

Attack (Opportunity Action): Melee 1 (triggering creature); +13 vs. Fortitude

Hit: 2d10 + 6 lightning damage. On a critical hit, the target is stunned (save ends).

Miss: Half damage.

Countermeasures

Disable: Thievery DC 26 Success: One tile is disabled. Failure (21 or less): The trap attacks the creature.Allies can use Thievery or Arcana to aid the Thievery check, using the aid another action.

Published in Dungeon Master's Kit, page(s) 220.
