Drow Stinger
Medium fey humanoid, drow
Level 6 Skirmisher XP 250

HP 73; Bloodied 36 Initiative +10
AC 20, Fortitude 17, Reflex 19, Will 17 Perception+9
Speed 6 Darkvision

Standard Actions

Short Sword (weapon) At-Will

Effect: The drow shifts up to 3 squares before or after the attack.

Attack: Melee 1 (one creature); +11 vs. AC

Hit: 2d6 + 8 damage, or 3d6 + 8 if the drow has combat advantage against the target.

Sword Flourish Recharge

Effect: The drow uses short sword twice, then gains a +2 power bonus to AC until the start of its next turn.

Minor Actions

Cloud of Darkness (zone) Encounter

Effect: Close burst 1. The burst creates a zone that lasts until the end of the drow’s next turn. The zone blocks line of sight for all creatures except the drow. While entirely within the zone, any creature other than the drow is blinded.
