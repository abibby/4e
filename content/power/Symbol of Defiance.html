Runepriest Utility 22Symbol of Defiance

The symbol of defiance fortifies your allies when your foes believe they have defeated you.

Encounter        Divine
Minor Action      Close burst 5

Target: Each bloodied ally in the burst

Effect: You slide each target 5 squares. Until the end of your next turn, each target gains a power bonus to all defenses equal to your Wisdom modifier.


Published in Player's Handbook 3, page(s) 109.
