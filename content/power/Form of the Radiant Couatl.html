Radiant Serpent Attack 20Form of the Radiant Couatl

You take on a serpentine appearance as radiance surrounds you, granting you the movement and resilience of the great couatl.

Daily        Healing, Lightning, Polymorph, Primal, Radiant
Minor Action      Personal

Effect: You assume the guardian form of the radiant couatl until the end of the encounter. While you are in this form, you gain a fly speed equal to your speed, and you can hover. You gain a +5 bonus to saving throws against ongoing damage and against effects that immobilize, restrain, or slow you.
Once during this encounter, you can use the Form of the Radiant Couatl Attack power while you are in this form.


Form of the Radiant Couatl Attack

Encounter      Healing, Lightning, Polymorph, Primal, Radiant
Standard Action      Area burst 2 within 20 squares

Target: Each enemy in the burst

Attack: Strength + 4 vs. Reflex
Level 26: Strength + 6 vs. Reflex

Hit: 2d10 + Strength modifier lightning and radiant damage. One ally in the burst can spend a healing surge.

Miss: Half damage.

Requirement: The power Form of the Radiant Couatl must be active in order to use this power.
