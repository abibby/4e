Perception Utility 10Spot Weakness

You pinpoint a weakness and direct your next attack toward it.

Encounter
Minor Action      Ranged sight

Target: One creature

Effect: Until the end of your next turn, you gain a +4 power bonus to your next damage roll against the target.
