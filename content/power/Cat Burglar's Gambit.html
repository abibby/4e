Cat Burglar Attack 11Cat Burglar's Gambit

You spring into action, expertly strike, and then sidestep to position yourself for either certain glory or imminent doom.

Encounter        Martial, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Requirement: You must be wielding a light blade.

Effect: You shift up to 3 squares before making the attack.

Target: One creature

Attack: Dexterity vs. AC

Hit: 3[W] + Dexterity modifier damage.

Effect: You shift up to 5 squares to a square adjacent to the target.
