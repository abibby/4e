Psionic Celerity [Augment]

Heroic Tier
Prerequisite: Psionic Augmentation class feature
Benefit: Whenever you run or charge, you gain a +1 bonus to speed.
    During a move action or a charge, you can spend 1 power point to gain a +2 bonus to speed until the end of your turn.

Published in Psionic Power, page(s) 133.
