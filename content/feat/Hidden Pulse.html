Hidden Pulse

Heroic Tier
Prerequisite: Genasi, rogue, firepulse, Sneak Attack class feature
Benefit: If you have combat advantage against the target of your firepulse racial power, you can deal your Sneak Attack damage to the target. This use doesn’t count toward your use of Sneak Attack for this round.

Published in Martial Power 2, page(s) 136.
