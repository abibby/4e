Paladin Utility 22Gift of Resistance

You project an aura around yourself that shields your allies from damage just as it shields you.

Daily        Divine, Stance
Minor Action      Personal

Effect: You enter the gift of resistance stance. Until the stance ends, each ally within 5 squares of you has resistance 20 to any of the same damage types to which you have resistance. However, your enemies’ attacks ignore all your resistances while you are in this stance.


Published in Dragon Magazine 387, page(s) 69.
