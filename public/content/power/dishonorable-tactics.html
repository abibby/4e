Rogue Utility 16Dishonorable Tactics

After your attack, you duck back under cover and hide.

Encounter        Martial
Free Action      Personal

Trigger: You make an attack roll against an enemy from which you are hidden

Effect: You remain hidden from the enemy until the end of your next turn or until you no longer have superior cover or total concealment from the enemy.


Published in Martial Power 2, page(s) 66.
