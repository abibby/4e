Shaman Utility 2Spirit of Life

The spirit of a golden owl alights on your ally’s shoulder and flutters off, carrying with it that friend’s aches and wounds.

Daily        Healing, Primal
Standard Action      Close burst 10

Target: One ally in the burst

Effect: The target regains hit points as if he or she had spent a healing surge.
