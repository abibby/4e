Guardian of Two Worlds Utility 12Stand of the Protector

Those who stand near you are revived and refreshed by your stalwart presence.

Daily        Primal, Stance
Minor Action      Personal

Effect: At the start of your turn, when you use your Font of Life class feature, a single ally adjacent to you can also make a single saving throw against an effect that a save can end as a free action.
