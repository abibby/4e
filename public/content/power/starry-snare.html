Cleric Attack 17Starry Snare

You weave a net of astral light, which imprisons your foe in glittering strands.

Encounter        Divine, Implement, Radiant
Standard Action      Ranged 10

Target: One creature

Attack: Wisdom vs. Fortitude

Hit: 3d8 + Wisdom modifier radiant damage. Until the end of your next turn, the target is immobilized, cannot teleport, and does not benefit from being insubstantial.


Published in Divine Power, page(s) 38.
