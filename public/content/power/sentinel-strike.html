Cleric Attack 17Sentinel Strike

You imbue your weapon with divine power and then strike. Your foe is overcome by the power of your god, foiling its attacks against a chosen ally.

Encounter        Divine, Weapon
Standard Action      Melee weapon

Target: One creature

Attack: Strength vs. AC

Hit: 3[W] + Strength modifier damage. Choose one ally within 5 squares of you. Until the end of your next turn, the damage of the target’s attacks against that ally is reduced to 0.


Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 69, Class Compendium.
