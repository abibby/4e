Fighter Utility 6Kirre's Roar

You let out an explosive roar, diverting your enemies’ attentions to you. The call steels you against the imminent assault.

Encounter        Martial
Minor Action      Close burst 3

Target: Each enemy in the burst

Effect: You mark each target until the end of your next turn. In addition, you gain resistance to all damage equal to your Dexterity modifier until the end of your next turn.


Published in Dark Sun Campaign Setting, page(s) 87.
