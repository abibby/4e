Shaman Attack 23Spirit of the Death Raven

A black bird swoops over your foe—a sign of ill omen, signifying that your enemy’s death is at hand.

Encounter        Implement, Primal, Psychic
Standard Action      Close burst 10

Target: One creature

Attack: Wisdom vs. Will

Hit: 4d8 + Wisdom modifier psychic damage. Before the end of your next turn, you roll a d20 the next time the target is hit by an attack that isn’t a critical hit. If you roll 15 or higher, that attack becomes a critical hit.


Published in Player's Handbook 2, page(s) 129.
