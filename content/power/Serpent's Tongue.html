Bluff Utility 2Serpent's Tongue

Your lie twists in your foe’s mind like a snake, allowing you to hide the venom behind your next words.

Encounter
Free Action      Personal

Trigger: You fail a Bluff check against a creature

Effect: You gain a +5 power bonus to the next Bluff, Diplomacy, or Intimidate check you make against that creature before the end of your next turn.


Published in Dragon Magazine 389.
