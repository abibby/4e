Book of Five Truths (The First Truth)Level 8 Uncommon

Scribes recorded five truths on scrolls at the start of the Dawn War and then scattered the scrolls throughout the planes of existence. Each scroll grants its reader essential insight into the nature of being.

Legendary Boon        3,400 gp

Property

Gain a +2 item bonus to Insight checks and Perception checks.



Published in Dungeon Master's Guide 2, page(s) 143, Dungeon Magazine 210.
